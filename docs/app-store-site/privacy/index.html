Privacy policy

How HealthFolio handles your information

This policy explains how StarkLabs collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use the HealthFolio app and related services.

Effective: July 10, 2026

Information you provide

We collect account and contact information such as your name, email address, phone number when provided, sign-in identifier, and user ID. Health profiles can include date of birth, sex, blood group, unit preference, and family or managed-profile details.

Health data includes medical documents and images you upload or forward by email, document metadata, extracted readings, manually entered information, medications, reminders, care notes, assistant questions, exports, and sharing choices. A selected photo or file is used only when you choose it for HealthFolio.

How we use information

We use information for app functionality: authenticating your account, routing records to the selected profile, storing and displaying documents, extracting readings, showing trends, managing reminders, answering questions from your records, creating exports, enabling user-controlled sharing, providing support, preventing misuse, and maintaining service reliability.

HealthFolio does not sell personal information, serve third-party advertising, or use health data for cross-app tracking.

Service providers

We disclose only the information needed to service providers acting for HealthFolio. Supabase provides authentication, database, server-function, and private file-storage infrastructure. OpenAI processes medical files for OCR and structured extraction. Apple and Google process sign-in when you choose those providers. Email infrastructure processes documents you intentionally forward to your HealthFolio address.

These providers process information under their own security and privacy obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Sharing you control

Family access, exported summaries, and doctor-share links are initiated by you. Anyone who receives an active share link may view the information included in it until the link expires, reaches its configured limit, or you revoke it. Review recipients before sharing sensitive health information.

Consent and your choices

HealthFolio asks for explicit consent before processing account and health data. You can review the recorded consent version in the app. Because HealthFolio cannot operate an account without processing this information, withdrawing consent requires account deletion. See Privacy choices for the exact in-app path and other controls.

Retention and account deletion

We retain account and health information while your account is active and as needed to provide requested features. Doctor-share links have configured expiry periods. A signed export download link expires after a short period, while its generated export file remains associated with the account until it is removed during account deletion. Local reminder and assistant data remains on your device until cleared or the account is deleted.

In-app account deletion permanently removes your authentication account, profile, health records, readings, unclaimed managed profiles, share links, exports, and stored files associated with the deleted account. Independently claimed family accounts and their records are preserved. Limited records may remain where retention is required for legal compliance, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or technically time-limited backups, and are not used for ordinary app functionality.

Security and international processing

We use access controls, private storage, encrypted network connections, signed file links, and administrative safeguards designed to protect information. No service can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards.

Children

HealthFolio is intended for users age 16 and older. A parent, guardian, or authorized caregiver may manage a family profile where permitted by law. Contact us if you believe a child provided information without appropriate authorization.

Medical disclaimer

HealthFolio is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, emergency services, dosing decisions, or clinical decision support. OCR and generated summaries can be incomplete or incorrect. Verify information against the original document and consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy when HealthFolio or legal requirements change. Material changes may require renewed in-app consent. Questions, corrections, access requests, or deletion help can be sent to starklabs2026@gmail.com.

Use of HealthFolio is also subject to Apple's Standard EULA.
