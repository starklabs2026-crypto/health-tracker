# Regulated Medical Device Declaration

App Store Connect may request this declaration because Medical or Treatment Information is frequent.

- United States: `No`
- United Kingdom: `No`
- European Union / EEA: `No`

Explanation for App Review:

> HealthFolio is a personal record-organization app. It stores user-selected medical documents, extracts text and readings for review, displays source/reference information, manages user-entered reminders, and creates user-controlled summaries. It does not diagnose a condition, recommend treatment, determine medication dosing, provide emergency services, control medical hardware, or replace a qualified healthcare professional. OCR and generated summaries are presented with verification and medical disclaimers.

Do not claim FDA, MHRA, CE, or other medical-device clearance unless StarkLabs obtains and documents it.
