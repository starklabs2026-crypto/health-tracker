Privacy Support

Privacy choices

Your information stays under your control

Use these controls to review, correct, export, share, restrict, or delete HealthFolio data.

Review or correct profile information

Open Profile to update supported account and health-profile fields. Verify extracted readings against the original medical document and replace an incorrect upload when necessary.

Export and sharing

You can create a health summary from Profile and use the system share sheet. Doctor share links can be reviewed and revoked in Profile. Remove access when a recipient no longer needs it.

Device permissions

Camera, photo-library, notification, and Face ID permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Turning off a permission disables the related feature but does not delete data already submitted to HealthFolio.

Withdraw consent and delete your account

HealthFolio needs consent to process account and health data. To withdraw consent, permanently delete the account in the app at:

Profile > Privacy & consent > Delete account and data

Review the deletion summary, acknowledge that deletion cannot be undone, then confirm. The app removes the account and associated HealthFolio data after the server confirms deletion. Independently claimed family accounts are not deleted with the owner account.

Need help with a privacy request?

Email starklabs2026@gmail.com with the subject “HealthFolio Privacy Request.” Do not attach medical records unless support specifically requests them.
