HealthFolio Privacy Policy

HealthFolio is a personal health record app for storing, organizing, and sharing user-provided health documents and readings.

Last updated: May 31, 2026

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide or generate while using HealthFolio, including account identifiers, contact details, authentication information, health profile details, uploaded health documents, document metadata, extracted readings, manually entered readings, family profile information, doctor-share links, and support messages.

If you use camera, photo library, document picker, biometric unlock, Apple sign-in, or Google sign-in features, HealthFolio uses those permissions only to provide the requested app functionality.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure your account, store and display your health records, process uploaded documents, extract health readings, show trends, manage family access, create limited doctor-share views, provide support, maintain app reliability, and protect against misuse.

HealthFolio does not use your health data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

Sharing And Disclosure

We share information only as needed to operate HealthFolio, including with service providers that host the app, store data, process authentication, store files, send messages, run OCR, or help us maintain security and reliability.

Doctor-share links and family access are controlled by users inside the app. Anyone with a valid doctor-share link may be able to view the health summary included in that link until it expires, is revoked, or reaches its configured use limit.

Health Data And Medical Disclaimer

HealthFolio is for personal record keeping and organization. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, treatment recommendation system, emergency service, or substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Data Retention And Deletion

We retain account and health record information for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide HealthFolio, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. You can request account deletion, data export, or correction by contacting support at starklabs2026@gmail.com.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal and health information. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, so you should use a strong account sign-in method and keep your device protected.

Children

HealthFolio is not directed to children under 13. Family profile features are intended to be managed by a parent, guardian, or authorized caregiver where applicable.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as HealthFolio changes. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date and may provide additional notice inside the app or through other appropriate channels.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact starklabs2026@gmail.com.
