Account Access

If you cannot sign in, include the email address or phone number used for your HealthFolio account and describe what happens after requesting your sign-in code.

Documents And OCR

For upload or extraction issues, include the document type, file format, approximate upload time, and whether the issue happened with camera, photo library, or file upload.

Health Readings

For incorrect or missing readings, include the parameter name, date, unit, and a short description of the expected result.

Family And Sharing

For family profile or doctor-share link issues, include the invite or share flow you used, but do not send sensitive health details unless support asks for them.

Privacy And Data Requests

To request account deletion, data export, correction, or privacy help, contact starklabs2026@gmail.com. Use the subject line “HealthFolio Privacy Request” so we can route it correctly.

Emergency Notice

HealthFolio does not provide emergency services, diagnosis, treatment advice, or clinical decision support. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional immediately.
