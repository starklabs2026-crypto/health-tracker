Sign-in and consent

Include the sign-in method you used and the exact message shown. Never send a password, one-time code, or Apple/Google credential.

Files, photos, and OCR

HealthFolio accepts supported medical PDFs and images. For a failed file, include the file type, approximate upload time, and whether it came from Files, Photos, camera, or forwarded email. Do not email the medical file unless support requests it.

Email ingestion

Confirm that you forwarded the message to the address shown in your HealthFolio profile and that the attachment is a supported medical document. Include the send time and email subject without sensitive medical details.

Sharing and exports

Describe whether the issue affects a doctor-share link, family profile, or exported summary. Revoke any link that reached an unintended recipient.

Account deletion and privacy

Delete an account in the app at Profile > Privacy & consent > Delete account and data For deletion trouble, correction, access, or export help, email support with the subject “HealthFolio Privacy Request.”

Medical emergency notice

HealthFolio does not provide emergency services, diagnosis, or treatment advice. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional immediately.
